Why this matters

One console language keeps the whole homelab easier to run

Shared spacing, color semantics, typography, and interaction rules make each service feel like part of one environment. That consistency improves trust, reduces context switching, and makes custom internal tools easier to build over time.

  • Consistent alert colors reduce misreads during incidents.
  • Shared panel, table, and form styles speed up internal app design.
  • Stable tokens make future theming and service-specific adaptations easier.
