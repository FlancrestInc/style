## Design Context

### Users
This showroom is for a single homelab owner comparing design directions for self-hosted services. The job is to evaluate colors, typography, spacing, and page patterns so future homelab dashboards and tools feel unified, pleasant to use, and fun over long sessions.

### Brand Personality
The experience should feel polished, smooth, and premium. Motion can be playful, but it should still feel controlled and intentional rather than flashy or noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
The visual system should help compare complete brand directions for homelab services, with enough personality to feel memorable while staying credible as an operations UI. The interface should avoid slow or heavy effects and prioritize fluid, high-performance interactions.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize cohesion so every service can feel like part of one family.
2. Keep interactions smooth and premium, with motion used to clarify and delight.
3. Preserve performance by favoring lightweight, transform-based effects over expensive animation.
4. Make comparison easy by highlighting theme changes and shared component behavior clearly.
5. Respect accessibility with reduced-motion fallbacks and readable, low-friction presentation.
